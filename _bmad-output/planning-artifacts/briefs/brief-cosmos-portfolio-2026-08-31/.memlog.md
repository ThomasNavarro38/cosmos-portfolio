- (event) STRUCTURE REVEALED AS A GRAPH, NOT A TREE: company<->client is many-to-many (a company links 1..n clients, a client links 1..n companies); client->project is one-to-many (project has exactly ONE client); project<->skill/tech is many-to-many. This CONTRADICTS the brainstorm's 'no parent + no duration' absence-classification rule, which presumes a single-parent hierarchy.
- (decision) GRAPH EXPRESSED AS ON-DEMAND GRAVITATIONAL CAPTURE (user-invented, supersedes coach options 1-5): skills are not statically placed. On focusing a project, its related skills are pulled in to ORBIT that project body. The many-to-many graph resolves per-focus - a skill can orbit project A now and project B later because it is never permanently anywhere. Same force system as the layout and the collapse; no new visual language, no drawn network lines. Preserves realism (no sci-fi connector lines) and awe (physical, animated, not a UI panel).
- (event) Coach flagged: the word 'magically' conceals the hard part - instantaneous teleport-in breaks the realism non-negotiable. Transition physics (where skills rest when unfocused, how they travel, how they leave) is UNRESOLVED and is the crux of this mechanic.
- (decision) SKILLS AS INTERSTELLAR MEDIUM (option 1) + DECAY DEPARTURE: skills exist permanently as diffuse dust - always present, unresolved, everywhere; explains the empty space between bodies. On focusing a project its skills CONDENSE out of the dust into orbiting bodies; on losing focus they DECAY back into dust. Nothing ever spawns or teleports, so realism holds. Symmetric arrival/departure - condensation and decay are the same process reversed, which also rhymes with the site's backward-time spine and the final collapse.
- (decision) DOMAIN MODEL CLARIFIED - Thomas is a CONSULTANT/CONTRACTOR, not a direct employee. 'Company' = intermediate/consultancy that holds both the client contract and Thomas's contract (the payer). 'Client' = the FINAL client company where the work actually happened. A client can be reached via multiple different intermediates over time, which is what makes company<->client many-to-many. The relationship is CONTRACTUAL, not containment.
- (decision) CLIENTS ARE THE STARS. Projects orbit clients. Intermediate/consultancy companies DEMOTE from bodies to an attribute of the project (label/tint/annotation) - they are billing infrastructure, not substance. This dissolves the company<->client many-to-many entirely: the company no longer needs a position, so the only remaining many-to-many is project<->skill, already solved by the dust/condensation mechanic. Celestial hierarchy is now a clean tree again: client(star) -> project(planet) -> skills(condensed from dust on focus).
- (decision) NO NDA CONSTRAINT - clients can be named. Real client names ship. Avoids anonymised-stars design problem entirely; named real companies are the credibility payload for recruiters/leads.
- (decision) PROJECT INFORMATION LAYER - fixed field set, identical for every project (uniform treatment, no hero/minor tiers): project name or quick description | Thomas's ROLE | short description of what he built | TEAM SIZE | short list of noticeable things worked on. Uniformity chosen deliberately: consistent schema, no per-entry curation decisions, nothing to rot.

- (decision) 'NOTABLE THINGS' FIELD = PROBLEM / DECISION / OUTCOME narrative, NOT a technology list. This is the field that carries what a CV structurally cannot: how Thomas thinks. Technologies are NOT in this field - they are a separate relation (project -> skills/technologies) already handled by the dust-condensation mechanic.
- (event) CONSTRAINT: no measurable outcomes, scale figures or before/after numbers available for any project. Evidence must be carried by the DECISION and the reasoning, not by metrics. Coach flagged the risk that outcomes collapse into unfalsifiable claims without numbers.
- (decision) NARRATIVE STRUCTURE CHANGED from problem/decision/OUTCOME to PROBLEM / CONSTRAINT / DECISION. Rationale: metrics were never measured or not retained, and unfalsifiable outcome claims ('more maintainable', 'improved performance') read as padding to the exact technical reader this site targets. The CONSTRAINT is the falsifiability substitute - a specific unfakeable condition (e.g. 'on-prem, no container runtime, had to ship as a single JAR') proves presence and reveals judgment without needing a number. Also truer to consulting: the consultant often leaves before the outcome is visible.
