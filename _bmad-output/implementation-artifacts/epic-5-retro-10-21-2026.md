- Epic 4's retro carried this explicitly. SR-3 said "`/colophon` renders a lone `<h1>the model</h1>` with no way out but Back … 5.1 must add both" (`epic-4-retro-10-19-2026.md:135`, `:208`).

- Effect: a visitor who arrives on a shared `/colophon` URL has no in-page route to the scene or the chart. The page has no chrome at all (no identity line, no route line).
- **Disposition:** fix now.
- **Prevention:** the handoff was written only in the retro's *Open questions*, not in `deferred-work.md` or as an AC. The Epic 3 pull-in rule (`epic-3-retro-item-7`) reads deferred-work.md alone. A carried retro obligation should be filed where the next story's spec creation actually reads.
