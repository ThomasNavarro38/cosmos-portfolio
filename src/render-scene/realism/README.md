# src/render-scene/realism/

The realism layer's home, and the `realism` chunk (7.5). The chunk decodes the maps the artifact names, draws one fill per Planet from them (7.6), and draws one fill and one glow per Star (7.7). They appear at once when the maps have decoded: 7.8 adds warm-up and the fade.

**Imports:** three, `../../tokens/tokens.ts` (`realism-ambient-floor-dark`, `realism-star-glow-dark`), `../scene-model.ts` (`FILL_RENDER_ORDER`, `GLOW_RENDER_ORDER`), `../stroke-model.ts` (`PLANET_RADIUS`, `STAR_RADIUS`) and a type from `../realism-status.ts`. `fill-model.ts` and `star-model.ts` import types only. No file here names the visitor's motion preference or `motionPace`: the mount hands the pace over as a number. Never `src/derive/`: the artifact names the maps, so the library stays the solver's. `budgets.ts` imports nothing and re-exports nothing.
**Imported by:** `../realism-mount.tsx` alone, by one `import('./realism/index.ts')`, a test of its own directory's modules, and `spikes/realism-fills/main.tsx`, the facade alone, which ships nowhere. `scripts/` may read `budgets.ts`, and nothing else here. `scripts/bundle-assertion.test.ts` holds that, and `scripts/check-maps.test.ts` holds the budgets' reach. No module in this directory may re-export `budgets.ts`.

**The chunk and its budgets.** `realism` is the chunk whose facade is `index.ts`, and `npm run build` prints both figures. `REALISM_CHUNK_BYTES_BUDGET` (24,000) counts its chunk plus its static closure outside the scene's. `SCENE_CHUNK_BYTES_BUDGET` (960,000) counts the closure of the chunk holding `scene.tsx` minus the closure of the chunk holding `mount.tsx`, since those load anyway. Three stays in what `scene` counts: `checkRealism` in `scripts/bundle-assertion.ts` fails if it lands in the mount's closure, under `realism` alone, or in any chunk `scene` does not count. It also fails if any module here is in the static closure of the scene chunk or the entry. The measured figures are in `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/realism-failure-7-5.md`.

## The fill's rules (7.6)

- **The depth mechanism is 7.1's, exactly.** `vec4(position.xy * radius, 0, 1)` through `modelViewMatrix`, then `gl_Position.z += 1e-6 * gl_Position.w`, render order `FILL_RENDER_ORDER`, transparent pass, opacity 1, depth write and test on, `FrontSide`. `1e-6` was measured only on a disc flattened to z = 0: keeping the sphere's relief would not hold at this value.
- **The disc faces the camera,** by copying its world quaternion each frame, which is what drei's `Billboard` computes. So its edge is a circle concentric with the outline, on the outline's own 64-gon.
- **One light, which never moves.** A world-space unit vector from a Planet to its own Star, from solved positions, written once at the build. Spin and tilt move only the map. There is no scene light, and no Body shadows another.
- **Spin holds on `reduced`,** handed in by the mount, and resumes from where it held. It does not use `motionPace`.
- **The tilt is about world `x`,** so a surface does not slide as the camera moves. The artifact's comment says "the Billboard's horizontal": they agree only when the camera faces along `z`.
- **Nothing is tone-mapped.** The map is `SRGBColorSpace`, the fragment includes `<colorspace_fragment>`, and `flat` stays.
- **The ceiling.** Materials normalise to `appearance.meanLuminance`. No fragment's luminance passes `stroke-active-dark`'s, a constant a test pins against the token. A saturated pixel may carry one channel above the token's. A pixel at `FILL_NEAR_CEILING` of the ceiling or above is floored to its 8-bit step, because rounding read one back above it.
- **Colour encodes nothing.** No module here or in the mount reads `luminanceBand`, `mass`, `isDeep` or `moteTier`. `appearance.meanLuminance` describes a map, is read by name, and is the one brightness from the artifact.
- **The images stay.** `dispose` frees the GPU's copies and closes no bitmap. A later Canvas builds from them. After a lost context three uploads them again on its next draw, so `uploaded` stays true. 7.8 must revisit that when it adds explicit uploads.
- **The seam and the pole are unmeasured on screen.** Longitude takes the smoother of two candidates, and is 0 at the exact pole, where `atan` is undefined.

`FILL_RADIUS_RATIO` is the final value of the recheck, `FILL_TARGET_LUMINANCE` is a starting value for Epic 8, and 7.9 may lower either.

## A Star's rules (7.7)

- **Two draws, on the one sphere.** `addedDraws(planets, stars)` is `planets + 2 × stars`, and Dust adds none. No second geometry and no plane.
- **The fill is the Planet's mechanism, exactly:** the same vertex stage, at `FILL_RADIUS_RATIO × STAR_RADIUS`, `FILL_RENDER_ORDER`, transparent pass, opacity 1, depth write and test on, `FrontSide`. It has no map, reads no `appearance`, and is unlit: the token's colour taken to a target luminance, darker toward the edge.
- **The glow is additive and writes no depth,** at `GLOW_RENDER_ORDER`, with `depthTest` on and no bias, coloured by `realism-star-glow-dark` alone. Its falloff, `(1 - r²)²`, reaches 0 with no slope at its edge, so no shape is cut out. Its size is the mesh's scale, not a uniform, so three culls it by what it covers: a glow reaches past the unit sphere a fill fits inside.
- **A glow is beneath every fill, including a farther one,** so a farther fill paints over a nearer Star's glow. AD-19's draw order stands, reversing it needs a ruling, and it is measured in `realism-glow-7-7.md` for Thomas's judgement in Epic 8.
- **The subject.** A Star rises only when its own ID equals `sceneSubject`. On the Rail that is the Star the camera is heading for. In free flight to a Client, `sceneSubject` returns its `flightTarget`, so that Star rises; only the free-flight overview has no subject, and nothing rises there. In Focus the subject is a Planet, so no Star rises and white is spent once. An ID that is no Star here raises nothing. Free flight was not measured on screen.
- **The rise is one value per Star, 0 to 1,** shared by its two draws. `nextRise` steps it over `RISE_SECONDS × pace`, with the frame's delta clamped so a stall cannot cut, a delta that is not positive and finite holds it, and a pace that is not one steps at full motion, so a falling Star always reaches rest. A layer's first frame seeds it (subject 1, others 0), since arrival is 7.8's. `riseShape` eases what the shaders read.
- **The pace comes through the mount** as a number, as `focus-panel.tsx` and `corner-chrome.tsx` compute it, so reduced motion shortens the rise and never cuts it. Spin still takes no pace.
- **The ceilings.** "At rest" means settled: once every rise has arrived, nothing here passes `stroke-active-dark`'s luminance, and the subject's Star stays below `stroke-focus-dark`'s, both constants a test pins against the tokens. **A falling Star passes above the rest ceiling for a moment,** because it leaves at the subject's brightness and the AC forbids a cut: its centre read above it for 21 frames, 0.35 s, at full motion and 5 frames, 0.083 s, reduced, on the dev machine. It never passes `stroke-focus`.
- **Overlapping glows are bounded only where sampled.** Additive blending is on encoded values and a shader cannot read the destination, so a test bounds a glow's own peak, and two at rest. Three rest peaks summed on encoded values decode above the rest ceiling, so the arithmetic does not hold for three. On the committed layout, a Rail walk at 60 samples a stop, every stop and Focus found at most two glows overlapping, with no pixel over either ceiling (at rest the largest read 0.4536 against 0.47119). Only those sampled poses hold this; a new layout or a pose between them may not. Pixels of the marks, at their token values, are not fill or glow pixels.
- **A Star lights nothing.** No Planet's lighting, Dust or the vignette changed, and there is no bloom, flare, composer, render target or full-screen pass. `GLOW_RADIUS_RATIO`, the targets and the peaks are starting values for Epic 8, and 7.9 may lower them.
