# src/render-scene/realism/

The realism layer's home, and the `realism` chunk (7.5). The chunk decodes the maps the artifact names, draws one fill per Planet from them (7.6), and draws one fill and one glow per Star (7.7). Once the maps have decoded, the layer is uploaded and compiled off-screen, drawn once hidden, and faded in (7.8).

**Imports:** three, `../../tokens/tokens.ts` (`realism-ambient-floor-dark`, `realism-star-glow-dark`), `../scene-model.ts` (`FILL_RENDER_ORDER`, `GLOW_RENDER_ORDER`), `../stroke-model.ts` (`PLANET_RADIUS`, `STAR_RADIUS`) and a type from `../realism-status.ts`. `fill-model.ts` and `star-model.ts` import types only, and `arrival-model.ts` imports nothing. No file here names the visitor's motion preference or `motionPace`: the mount hands the pace over as a number. No file here names `resetFrameSampling` or `chart-offer.ts`: the owner's port is the mount's (7.8). Never `src/derive/`: the artifact names the maps, so the library stays the solver's. `budgets.ts` imports nothing and re-exports nothing.
**Imported by:** `../realism-mount.tsx` alone, by one `import('./realism/index.ts')`, a test of its own directory's modules, and `spikes/realism-fills/main.tsx`, the facade alone, which ships nowhere. `scripts/` may read `budgets.ts`, and nothing else here. `scripts/bundle-assertion.test.ts` holds that, and `scripts/check-maps.test.ts` holds the budgets' reach. No module in this directory may re-export `budgets.ts`.

**The chunk and its budgets.** `realism` is the chunk whose facade is `index.ts`, and `npm run build` prints both figures. `REALISM_CHUNK_BYTES_BUDGET` (24,000) counts its chunk plus its static closure outside the scene's. `SCENE_CHUNK_BYTES_BUDGET` (960,000) counts the closure of the chunk holding `scene.tsx` minus the closure of the chunk holding `mount.tsx`, since those load anyway. Three stays in what `scene` counts: `checkRealism` in `scripts/bundle-assertion.ts` fails if it lands in the mount's closure, under `realism` alone, or in any chunk `scene` does not count. It also fails if any module here is in the static closure of the scene chunk or the entry. The measured figures are in `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/realism-failure-7-5.md`.

## The fill's rules (7.6)

- **The depth mechanism is 7.1's, exactly.** `vec4(position.xy * radius, 0, 1)` through `modelViewMatrix`, then `gl_Position.z += 1e-6 * gl_Position.w`, render order `FILL_RENDER_ORDER`, transparent pass, opacity 1, depth write and test on at rest (7.8: depth write from the frame the fade reaches 1), `FrontSide`. `1e-6` was measured only on a disc flattened to z = 0: keeping the sphere's relief would not hold at this value.
- **The disc faces the camera,** by copying its world quaternion each frame, which is what drei's `Billboard` computes. So its edge is a circle concentric with the outline, on the outline's own 64-gon.
- **One light, which never moves.** A world-space unit vector from a Planet to its own Star, from solved positions, written once at the build. Spin and tilt move only the map. There is no scene light, and no Body shadows another.
- **Spin holds on `reduced`,** handed in by the mount, and resumes from where it held. It does not use `motionPace`.
- **The tilt is about world `x`,** so a surface does not slide as the camera moves. The artifact's comment says "the Billboard's horizontal": they agree only when the camera faces along `z`.
- **Nothing is tone-mapped.** The map is `SRGBColorSpace`, the fragment includes `<colorspace_fragment>`, and `flat` stays.
- **The ceiling.** Materials normalise to `appearance.meanLuminance`. No fragment's luminance passes `stroke-active-dark`'s, a constant a test pins against the token. A saturated pixel may carry one channel above the token's. A pixel at `FILL_NEAR_CEILING` of the ceiling or above is floored to its 8-bit step, because rounding read one back above it.
- **Colour encodes nothing.** No module here or in the mount reads `mass`, `isDeep` or `moteTier`, and none but `star-model.ts` reads `luminanceBand` by name (9.4: a Star's light carries its Client's recency band, AD-20 as amended; a Planet's fill still reads none). `star-layer.ts` takes the band through the plan, as `plan.band`, and never reads the field. `appearance.meanLuminance` describes a map, is read by name, and is the one brightness a fill takes from the artifact. A Star's `appearance.pulsePhase` comes from its ID and means nothing.
- **The images stay.** `dispose` frees the GPU's copies and closes no bitmap. A later Canvas builds from them. After a lost context three uploads them again on its next draw, so `uploaded` stays true. 7.8 revisited that with its explicit uploads and left it so: the warm-up is a new Canvas's, and a restore under one Canvas needs no request and no fade.
- **The seam and the pole are unmeasured on screen.** Longitude takes the smoother of two candidates, and is 0 at the exact pole, where `atan` is undefined.

`FILL_RADIUS_RATIO` is the final value of the recheck, `FILL_TARGET_LUMINANCE` is a starting value for Epic 8, and 7.9 may lower either.

## A Star's rules (7.7)

- **Two draws, on the one sphere.** `addedDraws(planets, stars)` is `planets + 2 × stars`, and Dust adds none. No second geometry and no plane.
- **The fill is the Planet's mechanism, exactly:** the same vertex stage, at `FILL_RADIUS_RATIO × STAR_RADIUS`, `FILL_RENDER_ORDER`, transparent pass, opacity 1, depth write and test on at rest, following the layer's one depth switch (7.8), `FrontSide`. It has no map, reads no `appearance`, and is unlit: the token's colour taken to a target luminance, darker toward the edge.
- **The glow is additive and writes no depth,** at `GLOW_RENDER_ORDER`, with `depthTest` on and no bias, coloured by `realism-star-glow-dark` alone. Its falloff, `(1 - r²)²`, reaches 0 with no slope at its edge, so no shape is cut out. Its size is the mesh's scale, not a uniform, so three culls it by what it covers: a glow reaches past the unit sphere a fill fits inside.
- **A glow is beneath every fill, including a farther one,** so a farther fill paints over a nearer Star's glow. AD-19's draw order stands, reversing it needs a ruling, and it is measured in `realism-glow-7-7.md` for Thomas's judgement in Epic 8.
- **The subject.** A Star rises only when its own ID equals `sceneSubject`. On the Rail that is the Star the camera is heading for. In free flight to a Client, `sceneSubject` returns its `flightTarget`, so that Star rises; only the free-flight overview has no subject, and nothing rises there. In Focus the subject is a Planet, so no Star rises and white is spent once. An ID that is no Star here raises nothing. Free flight was not measured on screen.
- **The rise is one value per Star, 0 to 1,** shared by its two draws. `nextRise` steps it over `RISE_SECONDS × pace`, with the frame's delta clamped so a stall cannot cut, a delta that is not positive and finite holds it, and a pace that is not a positive finite number steps at full motion, so a falling Star always reaches rest. A layer's first frame, the hidden warm draw since 7.8, seeds it (subject 1, others 0); the arrival's fade is the layer's, not the rise's. `riseShape` eases what the shaders read.
- **The pace comes through the mount** as a number, as `focus-panel.tsx` and `corner-chrome.tsx` compute it, so reduced motion shortens the rise and never cuts it. Spin still takes no pace.
- **The ceilings.** "At rest" means settled: once every rise has arrived, nothing here passes `stroke-active-dark`'s luminance, and the subject's Star stays below `stroke-focus-dark`'s, both constants a test pins against the tokens. **A falling Star passes above the rest ceiling for a moment,** because it leaves at the subject's brightness and the AC forbids a cut: its centre read above it for 21 frames, 0.35 s, at full motion and 5 frames, 0.083 s, reduced, on the dev machine. It never passes `stroke-focus`. **During a subject change two Stars are partly white for a moment (9.4):** the core's hue goes toward white with the rise, so the falling and the rising Star are both partly white for up to `RISE_SECONDS`. The whitening is provisional: whether the subject's core turns white or keeps its tint is 9.5's open ruling (9.2's deferred finding).
- **Overlapping glows are bounded only where sampled.** Additive blending is on encoded values and a shader cannot read the destination, so a test bounds a glow's own peak, and two at rest. Three rest peaks summed on encoded values decode above the rest ceiling, so the arithmetic does not hold for three. On the committed layout, a Rail walk at 60 samples a stop, every stop and Focus found at most two glows overlapping, with no pixel over either ceiling (at rest the largest read 0.4536 against 0.47119, measured on 7.7's glows, before 9.4 changed the glow peaks by band; a re-measurement is owed in 9.11). Only those sampled poses hold this; a new layout or a pose between them may not. Pixels of the marks, at their token values, are not fill or glow pixels.
- **A Star lights nothing.** No Planet's lighting, Dust or the vignette changed, and there is no bloom, flare, composer, render target or full-screen pass. `GLOW_RADIUS_RATIO`, `GLOW_BAND_PEAK`, `GLOW_SUBJECT_PEAK` and 9.4's band ranges, dip, pulse, limb, grain and edge constants are starting values for 9.11.

## A Star's light (9.4)

Under realism a Star is its light, and its outline and core dot give way to it (AD-19, AD-20, AD-21 and AD-22 as amended).

- **Still two draws, on the one sphere:** the emissive core, which replaces 7.7's fill in the same draw, and the glow. `addedDraws` is unchanged.
- **The core is limb-darkened into its glow.** Its brightness falls from the centre to the edge by the linear law on `mu` (`STAR_CORE_LIMB` at the edge), and its alpha ramps toward 0 over the outer `STAR_CORE_EDGE` of its radius, so it melts into the additive glow beneath it (8.4's "dark ring"). **No fragment under 0.5 alpha draws or writes depth:** below `STAR_CORE_ALPHA_FLOOR` (0.5) a fragment is discarded, because once the layer writes depth a nearly invisible rim would otherwise cut later depth-tested draws (the line-work at `BODY 2`, Mote orbits, the belt and disc to come) out in a ring behind it. The soft ramp above the floor stays. `STAR_CORE_EDGE` is in `(0, 1)`, so `smoothstep` always has `edge0 < edge1`.
- **Recency is in the light.** A Star's centre at rest stays inside its band's range, `STAR_BAND_LUMINANCE`, from its Client's `luminanceBand` in the artifact. The ranges are disjoint and ordered, so a `stroke-near` Star's dimmest is above a `stroke-rest` Star's brightest at any clock, and both stay under `stroke-active`. The glow's rest peak follows the band too (`GLOW_BAND_PEAK`).
- **The pulse never leaves the band.** It is one slow sine of `STAR_PULSE_SECONDS`, phased from the Client's `appearance.pulsePhase`, moving the rest centre across the band's range.
- **The subject's centre alone reaches `stroke-focus`.** As the subject rises, the centre rises to `stroke-focus` and its hue goes to white at the same luminance (provisional, as above); each channel is then clamped to 1, which only lowers luminance, so a saturated tint (9.5) cannot clip. The subject's pulse only dims, by `STAR_SUBJECT_DIP` at most, so its peak is `stroke-focus` in linear terms at the analytic centre; on screen it approaches `stroke-focus` and never passes it, since the `FILL_NEAR_CEILING` floor lowers it by up to one 8-bit step. The limb's and the granulation's darkening is 0 at the centre alone (their factor is 1 there), so no other pixel reaches it. The ceilings hold every fragment as before, pinned against the tokens.
- **Granulation** dims only, by up to `STAR_GRAIN_DEPTH` weighted by radius, and drifts slowly with the one shared `uTime`. The clock steps by the frame's delta clamped to `RISE_MAX_FRAME_SECONDS`, so a stall cannot jump the pulse or the grain, and wraps at `STAR_CLOCK_WRAP_SECONDS` (400 pulses, an hour), so the float32 `uTime` keeps its precision. The pulse is continuous across the wrap; the granulation jumps once per wrap, which is accepted.
- **Reduced motion holds both.** The mount hands `reduced` to the layer, which hands it to the Stars' one clock (`nextClock`), as spin is. The clock holds, so the pulse and the granulation hold; the rise still steps. Nothing here names the preference.
- **The outline and the core dot fall on the one fade.** `fade()` reads the layer's one fade back. The owner's `lineWorkFade()` is `1 - fade` once the arrival has arrived, and 1 with no layer, while it warms, on the hidden warm draw, after failure, and on a fade read that throws. `body-field.tsx` reads `lineWorkFade()` in its own frame callback, which runs before the mount's because it is mounted earlier, so it reads the fade the layer reached on the previous frame, one frame late (and returns the marks one frame after the layer goes), which the spec accepts; it writes that value to each Star's billboard through `line-fade.ts`. No other line-work fades yet (9.10).
- **No tint yet.** The core and the glow keep `realism-star-glow-dark`; 9.5 adds the tints. The band ranges, dip, pulse period, limb, grain and edge are starting values for 9.11.

## The arrival's rules (7.8)

- **Warm before anything is seen.** `warm(renderer, camera, scene)` calls `initTexture` once per texture, then `compileAsync(group, camera, scene)` once, over the layer's own `Group` while it is in no scene, so R3F's next render cannot link the programs synchronously first, and against the scene it will join, so the programs are the ones that scene's draw looks up. A `Group` is not a draw. Then it reads each material's program `LINK_STATUS` straight from the context, which logs nothing, and rejects if any is not linked: three's own check runs on first use, and logs. `warm` attaches nothing.
- **The hidden warm draw.** The owner attaches the group at fade 0 on the frame after the warm-up resolves. At `uFade = 0` a fill blends to the destination unchanged under `NormalBlending` and a glow adds 0 under `AdditiveBlending`, so that frame binds every texture and program for real and shows nothing, with no render target or extra pass.
- **One fade.** One `{ value }` object, created in `fill-layer.ts` and shared by every Planet fill, Star fill and glow. A fill's alpha is `uFade`; a glow multiplies its colour by it. `arrive(true)` starts it stepping with each frame by `nextFade`, over `FADE_SECONDS × pace` with the delta clamped to `FADE_MAX_FRAME_SECONDS`, a bad delta holding it and a bad pace stepping at full motion; `arrive(false)` sets it to 1 at once. Fading only mixes a pixel toward what is already drawn, so the ceilings hold at every fade value.
- **Depth waits for 1.** Every fill is built with `depthWrite` false and switches on together on the frame the fade reaches 1, by `depthWrites`; with no fade, on the first visible frame. So while the layer fades, no line-work behind a fill vanishes, and once it has arrived a farther Body's marks behind a fill are hidden as in 7.7. A glow never writes depth.
- `FADE_SECONDS` (1.0) is a starting value, for Epic 8. The browser record is `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/realism-arrival-7-8.md`.

## The signal and the labels (7.9)

- **No fill hides a Ring or a Moon at a stop.** `signal.test.ts` projects, over the committed artifact at `DESKTOP_ASPECT` into 1920×1080, every Ring (64 samples on its circle) and every Moon (its centre and 16 rim points at its pixel-floored radius) at each integer Rail stop 0..N, with the camera `scene.tsx` builds, and fails naming the stop, the Body and the mark when a sample lies inside a nearer Body's fill disc, `FILL_RADIUS_RATIO × bodyRadius`, Planet and Star alike. Every fill, Ring and Moon is a disc parallel to the image plane, so each projects to a circle and the check is 2D, with the Body's view depth for "nearer". A Body's own fill never counts, glows write no depth and are never occluders, and points off screen or behind the camera are skipped. It fails too when no Ring and no Moon were tested. **It reads integer stops only:** hiding between stops, in transit, is accepted and not checked (AR-M3-16). It holds at rest, when fills write depth. Two controls show it can fail: a Body moved between the camera and the Ring, as a re-solve might, and an artifact with its Deep marks stripped.
- **The ceiling does not protect a label.** `ink-label-dark` is about 1.7:1 on `stroke-active-dark` and 5.85:1 on `surface-base-dark`; it keeps 4.5:1 only on a ground of relative luminance up to about 0.018, against `FILL_TARGET_LUMINANCE` 0.18. `signal.test.ts` states that from the tokens, through `src/shell/contrast.ts`.
- **Labels are measured on real pixels,** by `spikes/realism-fills/prod-labels.mjs`, against a `chunk-404` build at the same pose. The record is `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/realism-labels-7-9.md`. Over a lit Star the affordance's two lines fell to about 1:1, and only Stars put out at the ground could clear that from here. So, by Thomas's ruling, the affordance stands on a plate of `surface-base` (`affordance-model.ts`). The realism values are unchanged.

## The definition of done (7.10)

`done.test.ts` holds the AD-23 rows that nothing else broke both ways, each past its limit and each given nothing to measure. **Added draws:** the count and the layer's real draws over the committed artifact, which must hold at least one Planet and one Star, against a `fill-model.ts` mutant drawing three per Star. **The sphere:** the segments within 64×32, and exactly one `SphereGeometry` built from the constants, against mutants at 65 and 33 segments and a layer with no sphere. **The pipeline:** a scan over every shipped module under `src/` for a composer, a pass, a render target, a shadow map, a scene light or adaptive quality (`PerformanceMonitor`, `AdaptiveDpr`, `AdaptiveEvents`, `setDpr`, `regress(`), which fails on a walk that reads no module here. It also holds NFR-5 under realism: a Project appended to a copy of `career.json`, solved by the solve CLI in a child process (this directory imports nothing of `src/derive/`), has a library map and its mean, every prior appearance unchanged, and a fill lit by `lightDirection` toward its own Star. The chunk bytes, the maps and the dependencies are held by `scripts/`: `checkRealism`, `check-maps` and `check-pins`'s closed list. The whole audit, with M2's tests run against this tree, is `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/realism-done-7-10.md`.
